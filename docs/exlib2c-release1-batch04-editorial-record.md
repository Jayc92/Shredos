# EXLIB-2C Release-1 Batch 4 — editorial review record

Prepared 2026-08-28. Documents the complete line-by-line editorial
pass over all 25 Batch 4 records before commit. This record approves
nothing; every record remains pending with null review evidence.

## Checks performed

Every record was reviewed line-by-line for: exercise identity and
mechanics matching the named movement; setup/execution agreement;
primary prose matching the stable equipment metadata; laterality
consistency in every field including breathing wording; tracking-mode
wording (reps vs bodyweight vs cardio pace/effort); machine
instructions staying model-neutral while naming their adjustments;
Smith machine increment/hook/empty-bar rules; weighted-vest security;
platform, box, and bench stability; entry, exit, and
failure-management hazards; specific breathing without prolonged
holds or prescribed shallow breathing; observable-failure mistakes;
exercise-specific non-medical safety with concrete conservative
actions; accessibility alternatives that genuinely reduce a barrier;
defensible relationship direction with empty arrays preferred over
weak links; and duplicated/templated sentences within the batch and
against all 75 Batch 1-3 records.

## Batch-specific notes

- **Machine-heavy batch**: eleven records use machine equipment
  (including the stationary bike). All name their adjustments
  (seat, pad, handle, range, start-position, saddle, straps) and
  state that layouts differ between machines; none names a free
  weight in its primary prose.
- **Smith machine bench press and squat**: both set safety stops
  before loading (just below chest height for the press, just below
  the lowest squat depth for the squat), teach the rotate-to-re-hook
  bail at any height, require empty-bar hook familiarization, and
  carry the approved "next available increment/setting" progression
  wording with no fixed-increment prescription.
- **First weighted-vest records** (pull-up, push-up): the vest is
  fastened snugly so it cannot shift, work starts at the lightest
  vest setting, sets end earlier than unweighted work, and the
  pull-up dismounts by stepping down rather than dropping.
- **Bar-hang records** (Chin-up, Hanging knee raise, Hanging leg
  raise, Weighted vest pull-up): entry and exit use a stable,
  non-slip step or box; sets end while the grip is still secure;
  no record instructs dropping from height.
- **Nordic hamstring curl**: ankles anchored under a padded,
  immovable support; hands ready to catch; range starts with the
  top few degrees; the set stops before control is gone.
- **Ab wheel rollout**: rollout distance is capped by trunk control
  (end the set when the hips drop or the lower back starts to
  arch); kneeling is the standard exercise form here, not an
  accessibility fallback; the wall rollout serves as the gentler
  alternative.
- **Bulgarian split squat** is the batch's only unilateral record
  and follows the one-side-then-switch contract with singular
  wording in every field, including the breathing cue.
- **Stationary bike** covers the fixed-drive dismount hazard (wait
  for the pedals to stop), saddle height, and model-neutral
  resistance controls with cardio pace/effort/duration wording.
- **Dumbbell fly** teaches the thigh-start entry, keeps every
  knuckle wrapped because the load travels over the body, holds the
  elbow bend constant, and offers the floor version whose range is
  naturally limited.
