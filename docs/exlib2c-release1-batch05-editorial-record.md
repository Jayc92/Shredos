# EXLIB-2C Release-1 Batch 5 — editorial review record

Prepared 2026-08-28. Documents the complete line-by-line editorial
pass over all 25 Batch 5 records before commit. This record approves
nothing; every record remains pending with null review evidence.

## Checks performed

Every record was reviewed line-by-line for: exercise identity and
mechanics matching the named movement; setup/execution agreement;
primary prose matching the stable equipment metadata; laterality
consistency in every field including breathing wording; tracking-mode
wording; barbell rack-safety doctrine (functional clearance plus
unloaded-bar testing, never a generic height guarantee); Smith
increment/hook/empty-bar rules; load-security for bars resting on
the body; over-face load management; kettlebell and sandbag
failure-management (never catch, chase, or twist to save a moving
load); bar-hang entry/exit; vest security; platform stability;
specific breathing without prolonged holds or prescribed shallow
breathing; observable-failure mistakes; exercise-specific
non-medical safety with concrete conservative actions; accessibility
alternatives that genuinely reduce a barrier (kneeling only where
intrinsic, as in the cable crunch); defensible relationship
direction with empty arrays preferred over weak links; and
duplicated/templated sentences within the batch and against all 100
Batch 1-4 records.

   fail-closed generator scans, logged here rather than silently
   regenerated over):
   - Deadlift, safety — the draft phrase "treat every rep from the
     floor as its own lift" tripped the medical-language scan
     ("treat"); reworded to "take every rep from the floor as its
     own lift". Benign verb, but the scan is fail-closed by design
     and the wording is equivalent.

2. **Five line-by-line editorial corrections** (four caught by the
   post-generation read of the full draft, one by the focused
   verifier's stop/modify vocabulary check):
   - Front squat, equipment setup — the draft mentioned "small heel
     plates under a stable platform"; standing on loose plates is
     exactly the loose-plate-platform hazard family corrected in
     Batch 4, so the clause was removed in favor of rack re-testing
     and grip guidance.
   - Kettlebell clean and press, setup — a malformed draft clause
     ("learn both unloaded of fatigue") replaced with "learn the
     clean and the press fresh, before any fatigue sets in."
   - Overhead press, safety — the stall response ("ending the set
     beats grinding") did not carry one of the established concrete
     stop/modify actions; reworded to "end the set rather than
     grinding a bar overhead."
   - Straight-arm pulldown, accessibility — the draft offered a
     half-kneeling option; kneeling is not intrinsic to this
     movement, so the alternative is now the seated version only.
   - Kettlebell clean and press, safety — the bail instruction
     ("set it down") did not carry one of the established concrete
     stop/modify actions; reworded to "set the bell down and
     pause", keeping the no-catch rule verbatim.

## Batch-specific notes

- **Barbell-heavy batch**: ten records use a barbell. The two rack
  presses (Close-grip bench press, Incline barbell press) carry the
  promoted functional safety doctrine in exercise-specific wording:
  safeties at the highest position clearing the intended touch
  point, an unloaded-bar flatten-and-slide-clear test, range
  shortened when clearance is impossible, and a spotter as an
  addition to — never a substitute for — tested safeties. The
  incline press adds re-testing whenever the bench angle changes.
- **Front squat** bails by steering the bar down onto the tested
  safeties; the dropping-elbows warning is framed as the observable
  early signal to end the set.
- **Smith machine row** (third Smith record) sets tested stops just
  below the bar's hinged starting height, practices the hook
  rotation with an empty bar from the hinge, requires both hooks
  visibly caught before standing, and carries the approved "next
  available increment/setting" progression wording.
- **Hip thrust** requires a padded bar held with both hands so it
  cannot roll or slide, with the bench braced against a rack or
  wall — the load-security family from the Batch 4 review.
- **Skull crusher** keeps grinding out entirely: end a rep early,
  and exit by steering the bar to the chest and sitting up with it.
- **Kettlebell records** carry the no-catch discipline: a clean
  that misses returns to the rack or the floor, and a slipping
  swing finishes and parks early — never a grab at a moving bell.
- **Sandbag shouldering** alternates shoulders rep by rep and
  releases a sliding bag down and away rather than twisting to
  save it.
- **Bar-hang records** (Pull-up, Inverted row) pull-test the bar
  first, end sets while grip is secure, and exit via the stable
  step (Pull-up) or by walking the feet back under the body
  (Inverted row).
- **Weighted vest squat** re-tightens a shifting vest and removes
  it to finish sets when depth or knee control fades.
- **Unilateral records** (Cable lateral raise, Kettlebell clean and
  press, Pallof press, Single-leg glute bridge) use singular
  wording in every field and the one-side-then-switch-then-match
  contract; **alternating records** (Sandbag shouldering, Walking
  lunge) state their rhythm inside the set.
- **Cable crunch** kneels because kneeling is the exercise's
  working position — not an accessibility fallback — and its
  alternative is the floor crunch.
